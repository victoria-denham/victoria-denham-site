04 — About

Designer.
Communicator. Explainer.

I've spent over a decade working across small businesses, mission-led projects, advocacy work and creative education. The format changes, but the task is usually the same: taking something complex, scattered or hard to access and giving it a clearer public shape.

My background spans web and brand design, writing, visual communication, teaching, public speaking and accessible digital content. I like work that asks for both structure and feeling: the practical shape of a page, the right words in the right order, the image or short video that helps an idea land.

That's what I'm interested in. Helping useful, important or under-resourced work reach people more clearly, without losing the human part in the process.

✧ Background

✧ Teaching
